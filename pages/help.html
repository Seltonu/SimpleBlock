How to use SimpleBlock

SimpleBlock was created to help you stop endless scrolling.

Rules are intentionally flexible so you stay in control and decide when it's time to focus. We could make it harder to switch off, but then you wouldn't be the one in charge of your habits.

Three levels

  1. Global Turn EVERYTHING on or off
  2. Category Toggle categories you create
  3. Rule Individual block rules

Tips for adding rules

Block any page, Redirect to any link.

  • https://, www. and capital letters don't matter
  • Subdomains: news.google.com doesn't block mail.google.com, but google.com blocks both
